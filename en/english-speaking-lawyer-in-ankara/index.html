English-speaking lawyer in Ankara

Ankara is the administrative capital of Türkiye and many immigration and public-law matters involve authorities or courts located in the city. An English-speaking lawyer in Ankara can help a foreign client understand the procedure and the documents required for a specific matter.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

Ankara-based legal practice

Being based in Ankara can be particularly relevant when a matter involves central administrative institutions, Ankara courts or in-person procedural steps. The appropriate forum, however, depends on the specific legal act and should be checked rather than assumed.

Foreigners and immigration law

Common matters include residence permit applications and refusals, entry bans, removal decisions, immigration status questions and administrative litigation. Each case requires review of the actual decision and the date and method of notification.

Direct communication in English

Foreign clients can explain facts and documents in English. Legal options can then be presented in a structured way, including what documents are missing, what deadlines may apply and which authority is competent.

Court interpreting is a separate role

Court interpreting in English, Bulgarian, French and Russian is limited to assignments within the relevant Ankara judicial interpreter list. It should not be confused with general notarial, consular or embassy translation services.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be in Ankara to instruct a lawyer?

Not necessarily. Whether in-person attendance is required depends on the procedure and the documents involved.

Does being in Ankara change the legal deadline?

No. Deadlines come from the applicable law and the notification of the relevant decision, not from the lawyer's location.

Can you communicate in English before documents are translated?

Yes, factual discussion and preliminary review can be conducted in English; official document requirements are assessed separately.
