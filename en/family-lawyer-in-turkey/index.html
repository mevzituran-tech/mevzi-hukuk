Family lawyer in Turkey

International family matters require attention to jurisdiction, civil-status documents, the interests of children and any connection with another country.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. Client communication is available in English. This does not imply qualification in England, the United States, or another foreign jurisdiction.

Divorce and family disputes

The procedure depends on the facts, residence of the parties, available documents and the relevant procedural rules.

Children, custody and maintenance

The child's interests, actual care arrangements, financial evidence and other facts are central to many disputes involving custody and support.

Foreign documents

Marriage, birth or divorce records issued abroad may require an apostille, translation or another formality before use in Türkiye.

How to prepare the matter

For an initial assessment, collect the official decisions, notices, contracts, reports and correspondence, record the exact dates, and prepare a short chronology. If a document was issued outside Türkiye, it may be necessary to check separately whether an apostille, translation or another formality is required for its use in the relevant Turkish procedure.
