Administrative lawyer in Turkey

An administrative dispute starts with the actual decision: who issued it, on what legal basis, for what reasons and when it was served.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. Client communication is available in English. This does not imply qualification in England, the United States, or another foreign jurisdiction.

Reviewing the decision

The full act should be examined together with its reasoning, legal basis and factual findings.

Court and deadline

Different administrative acts may have different competent courts and procedural deadlines, so one template cannot be applied to every dispute.

Evidence and service

A clear chronology, official records and proof of service can be crucial in determining the appropriate procedural route.

How to prepare the matter

For an initial assessment, collect the official decisions, notices, contracts, reports and correspondence, record the exact dates, and prepare a short chronology. If a document was issued outside Türkiye, it may be necessary to check separately whether an apostille, translation or another formality is required for its use in the relevant Turkish procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Can every administrative decision be challenged?

Available remedies depend on the type of act and the applicable legislation.

When does the deadline start?

Service is often important, but the precise deadline must be determined from the specific act and legal framework.

Is an oral explanation from an official enough?

No. A written decision or official record is a much stronger basis for legal assessment.
