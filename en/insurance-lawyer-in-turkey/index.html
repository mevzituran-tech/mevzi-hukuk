Insurance lawyer in Turkey

Insurance disputes should be assessed through the policy, general terms, notice of loss, expert material and the insurer's stated reasons for denial.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. Client communication is available in English. This does not imply qualification in England, the United States, or another foreign jurisdiction.

Denied claims

The denial should be compared with the covered risk, exclusions and the factual circumstances of the event.

Proving loss

Photographs, expert assessments, invoices, incident records and correspondence may be important evidence.

Procedural options

Different insurance products and disputes may involve different pre-litigation or court mechanisms.

How to prepare the matter

For an initial assessment, collect the official decisions, notices, contracts, reports and correspondence, record the exact dates, and prepare a short chronology. If a document was issued outside Türkiye, it may be necessary to check separately whether an apostille, translation or another formality is required for its use in the relevant Turkish procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Is a short denial letter enough to assess the case?

Not always. It should be read together with the policy and the insurer's contractual basis.

What should be kept after a loss?

The policy, reports, photographs, expert records, invoices and correspondence.

Can payment be guaranteed?

No. The outcome depends on the contract, facts, evidence and applicable procedure.
