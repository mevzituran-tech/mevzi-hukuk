Entry ban lawyer in Turkey

An entry ban may become known at the border or during an immigration-status check. Legal analysis should begin by identifying the exact official restriction and its basis.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

Identify the official record

The key questions are who imposed the restriction, when it was imposed, for how long and under what legal basis. Official documents or records are more reliable than verbal explanations.

Collect the relevant immigration history

Passport records, prior residence permits, visa history, removal decisions and family documents can help explain why the restriction exists and what legal routes may be available.

Administrative and judicial options

Depending on the type of restriction, an administrative application, judicial challenge or a combination of steps may be considered after the record is verified.

Family and proportionality considerations

Family ties, children and previous lawful residence may be relevant in some cases, but their significance must be assessed within the legal framework of the specific restriction.

Frequently asked questions

Can an entry ban be removed by a simple letter?

Sometimes an administrative request may be relevant, but there is no universal method.

Do I need to know the restriction code?

The exact record and legal basis are highly useful for determining the correct route.

Can a lawyer guarantee removal of the ban?

No. The result depends on the legal basis, facts, evidence and decision of the competent authority or court.
