Deportation lawyer in Turkey

A removal decision can have serious consequences for a foreign national. The correct response depends on the legal ground, the written decision, the notification date and the individual's circumstances.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

Read the full decision

The first step is to identify the authority, date, legal basis and factual reasons stated in the removal decision. A short oral explanation is not a substitute for the document itself.

Preserve proof of notification

Envelope, receipt, electronic notification or other evidence of service should be kept. Notification may affect procedural deadlines.

Individual circumstances

Family life, children, length of stay, current immigration status and other factors may be relevant, but they must be assessed together with the legal ground for removal.

Judicial review

Where judicial protection is available, the procedure must be planned around the actual administrative act and applicable deadlines. There is no single strategy that fits every removal case.

Frequently asked questions

Can a removal decision be challenged?

Depending on the legal basis and facts, judicial or administrative remedies may be available.

What is the most important document?

The full removal decision and proof of notification are usually essential.

Does having family in Turkey automatically stop removal?

No. Family circumstances may be relevant, but they do not automatically determine the outcome.
