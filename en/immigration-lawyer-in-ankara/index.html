Immigration lawyer in Ankara

Foreigners law and immigration matters in Ankara often involve administrative authorities, central institutions or courts. Legal work should focus on the actual decision, notification, competent authority and available remedy.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

Residence and status issues

Applications, extensions and refusals should be reviewed in light of the legal basis relied upon by the administration and the consistency of the applicant's documents.

Administrative litigation

Some immigration disputes may require judicial review. The correct court and procedural route depend on the challenged administrative act and the applicable legislation.

Entry bans and removal decisions

These matters require careful attention to official records, legal grounds and notification dates. Informal explanations alone are usually insufficient for a complete legal assessment.

English communication for foreign clients

The client can communicate the facts and concerns in English while the legal analysis remains grounded in Turkish law and official documents.

Frequently asked questions

Can an Ankara lawyer handle a matter involving another city?

Potentially yes, depending on jurisdiction, procedure and whether local attendance is required.

What documents are most important?

The administrative decision, proof of notification, passport and immigration documents, and a clear chronology are usually central.

Do immigration cases have standard outcomes?

No. Outcomes depend on the legal basis, facts, evidence and the competent authority or court.
