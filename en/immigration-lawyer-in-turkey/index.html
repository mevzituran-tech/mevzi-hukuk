Immigration lawyer in Turkey

Immigration law in Türkiye covers much more than residence permit applications. It also includes administrative decisions, entry restrictions, removal proceedings, notification rules and judicial review.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

What an immigration-law assessment should cover

A proper assessment starts with the client's current legal status, the written administrative decision, notification details, travel and residence history, family circumstances and supporting documents.

Residence permits

Questions may concern first applications, extensions, refusals or inconsistencies in documents. The legal response depends on the stated reason for the decision and whether the issue can be corrected administratively or requires judicial review.

Entry bans and restriction records

An entry restriction should be examined through official information whenever possible. The exact record, authority, duration and legal basis determine whether an administrative request, litigation or another route may be appropriate.

Removal proceedings

Removal decisions can have immediate consequences. The written decision and proof of notification are particularly important because procedural deadlines may be short.

Frequently asked questions

Is every immigration matter solved by filing a new application?

No. In some cases the existing administrative decision must first be analysed or challenged.

Are family ties relevant?

They may be relevant, but their legal significance depends on the specific decision and supporting evidence.

Can immigration advice be given without seeing the decision?

Only at a very general level. A reliable assessment normally requires the written decision or official record.
