Criminal lawyer in Turkey

Criminal proceedings in Türkiye may begin with a complaint, investigation, summons for questioning, detention, or a prosecutor's file. The legal position should be built from the client's procedural status, the case materials and the available evidence.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. Client communication is available in English. This does not imply qualification in England, the United States, or another foreign jurisdiction.

Investigation and questioning

At an early stage, it is important to identify whether the person is a suspect, accused person, victim or complainant and what procedural documents already exist.

Detention and preventive measures

Where detention or a preventive measure is under consideration, timing and access to the relevant materials can be critical.

Court proceedings

Defence or representation should be based on the accusation, evidence, procedural records and court decisions. No outcome can properly be guaranteed.

How to prepare the matter

For an initial assessment, collect the official decisions, notices, contracts, reports and correspondence, record the exact dates, and prepare a short chronology. If a document was issued outside Türkiye, it may be necessary to check separately whether an apostille, translation or another formality is required for its use in the relevant Turkish procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Do you represent both defendants and victims?

Depending on the matter, legal work may involve defence of an accused person or representation of a victim or complainant.

Can acquittal be guaranteed?

No. A lawyer cannot properly guarantee the outcome of a criminal case.

What should I send first?

Summonses, interview records, prosecutor or court decisions, charging documents and a short chronology.
