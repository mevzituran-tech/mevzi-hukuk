Criminal lawyer in Ankara

When a criminal matter is being handled in Ankara, the key questions are the stage of the case, the investigating authority or court, the client's procedural status and the decisions already issued.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. Client communication is available in English. This does not imply qualification in England, the United States, or another foreign jurisdiction.

At the investigation stage

The complaint, suspicion, interview records and prosecution decisions should be reviewed together with any evidence available under the procedural rules.

Before the court

Defence strategy depends on the charge, evidence and procedural objections. For a victim, participation rights and possible applications are assessed separately.

English communication

A foreign client can discuss the facts and procedure in English while the legal analysis remains grounded in Turkish criminal law and procedure.

How to prepare the matter

For an initial assessment, collect the official decisions, notices, contracts, reports and correspondence, record the exact dates, and prepare a short chronology. If a document was issued outside Türkiye, it may be necessary to check separately whether an apostille, translation or another formality is required for its use in the relevant Turkish procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Can a lawyer enter the case after trial has started?

In many cases yes, subject to the current stage and the available file materials.

Do you work with foreign nationals?

Yes. Foreign status is considered together with the criminal-procedure issues of the case.

Is an interpreter needed in court?

That depends on the participant's language ability and the court's procedural requirements.
