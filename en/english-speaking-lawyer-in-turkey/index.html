English-speaking lawyer in Turkey

Foreign clients often need a Turkish lawyer who can explain the legal process clearly in English while handling the matter under Turkish law. MEVZİ provides English-language communication for legal matters involving foreigners in Türkiye, especially immigration and administrative issues.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

Legal assistance for foreigners in Türkiye

Foreign nationals may need legal support for residence permits, administrative decisions, entry restrictions, removal proceedings, family matters, inheritance, civil disputes or cross-border documentation. The first step is to identify the authority involved, the legal basis of the decision and any applicable procedural deadline.

Why English-language communication matters

Clear communication reduces misunderstandings about documents, deadlines and procedural choices. Clients can explain the factual background in English and receive a structured explanation of the Turkish legal process, while official filings and court proceedings remain subject to Turkish procedural rules.

Immigration and administrative matters

Residence permit refusals, entry bans and removal decisions can involve short procedural time limits and detailed administrative records. A useful legal assessment therefore begins with the written decision, notification documents and the client's current immigration status.

Cross-border matters

For matters involving Türkiye and Bulgaria, civil status records, inheritance documents, property records and other official documents may need to be coordinated across jurisdictions. The correct sequence of steps depends on the authority and purpose for which each document will be used.

Frequently asked questions

Is an English-speaking lawyer the same as an English lawyer?

No. “English-speaking lawyer” here means a Turkish lawyer who communicates with clients in English. It does not mean qualification as a solicitor or barrister in England and Wales.

Can a foreign client communicate entirely in English?

Client communication can be conducted in English, but Turkish authorities and courts operate under Turkish law and procedural language requirements.

What should I send before an initial legal assessment?

The relevant decision or notice, passport and immigration documents, key dates, and a short chronology of events are usually the most useful starting materials.
