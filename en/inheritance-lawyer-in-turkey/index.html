Inheritance lawyer in Turkey

An inheritance matter involving a foreign national may include property in Türkiye, heirs in different countries and official records issued abroad.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. Client communication is available in English. This does not imply qualification in England, the United States, or another foreign jurisdiction.

Identifying heirs

The first task is to determine the heirs and the documents proving death, family relationships and civil status.

Property and registries

Where real estate is involved, title records, ownership documents and possible encumbrances should be checked.

Cross-border documents

Foreign documents may need an apostille, official translation or another formality depending on their intended use in Türkiye.

How to prepare the matter

For an initial assessment, collect the official decisions, notices, contracts, reports and correspondence, record the exact dates, and prepare a short chronology. If a document was issued outside Türkiye, it may be necessary to check separately whether an apostille, translation or another formality is required for its use in the relevant Turkish procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreign national inherit property in Turkey?

The answer depends on the type of asset and the applicable legal rules.

What should be prepared first?

Civil-status records, inheritance documents, property records and other evidence of family relationship or ownership.

Can Türkiye–Bulgaria matters be handled?

Yes, where the matter involves Turkish procedure and cross-border documentation.
