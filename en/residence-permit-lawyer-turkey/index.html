Residence permit lawyer in Turkey

Residence permit matters can turn on the legal basis of stay, the consistency of documents and the reasons stated by the migration authority. A refusal should be examined through the written decision before choosing the next step.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

Applications and extensions

Passport information, address records, insurance, family documents and the stated purpose of stay should be consistent. Missing or contradictory information can affect the application.

After a refusal

The written decision should be obtained and reviewed carefully. The date and method of notification may also matter for any administrative or judicial remedy.

Correcting documents or challenging the decision

Not every refusal requires the same response. Depending on the reason, the appropriate step may be to correct documentation, make a new administrative submission or seek judicial review.

Family and humanitarian circumstances

Children, family ties, health-related factors or other individual circumstances may be legally relevant, but their weight depends on the applicable legal ground and evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Should I reapply immediately after refusal?

Not automatically. First identify why the application was refused and what legal consequences the decision has.

Is an oral explanation from the migration office enough?

No. The written decision is the better basis for legal assessment.

Should I keep the notification document?

Yes. It may be important for determining procedural deadlines.
