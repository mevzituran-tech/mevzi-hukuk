Lawyer for foreigners in Turkey

Foreign clients may face legal issues that combine immigration status, administrative decisions, family relationships, property or documents from more than one country. The legal work should begin by separating the issues and identifying the correct authority for each.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. English is used for client communication; legal proceedings in Türkiye are conducted under Turkish law and procedure.

Immigration and public-law matters

Residence permit refusals, entry bans and removal decisions require attention to the written decision, notification, deadlines and supporting documents.

Family, civil and inheritance matters

Foreign clients may also need legal assistance concerning divorce, custody, maintenance, inheritance, debt or property. Cross-border elements can affect document requirements and jurisdiction.

Türkiye–Bulgaria matters

Where a case involves both Türkiye and Bulgaria, civil status records, inheritance documents, property records and official translations may need to be coordinated in the correct procedural sequence.

Working in English

English-language communication can make the legal process easier to understand, but the legal analysis is based on Turkish law and the official documents relevant to the matter.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner hire a Turkish lawyer remotely?

Often yes, although some procedures may require formal powers of attorney or in-person steps.

What should a foreign client prepare first?

Official decisions, passport and immigration documents, relevant contracts or civil-status records, and a short chronology.

Does speaking English change the applicable law?

No. English is used for communication; Turkish law and procedure govern matters before Turkish authorities and courts.
