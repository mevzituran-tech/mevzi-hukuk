Commercial lawyer in Turkey

Commercial disputes in Türkiye often require close review of the contract, correspondence, invoices, payments and the parties' conduct before choosing litigation, enforcement or settlement.

Professional status: Mevzi Turan Hacıosmanoğlu is a Turkish lawyer registered with the Ankara Bar Association. Client communication is available in English. This does not imply qualification in England, the United States, or another foreign jurisdiction.

Contract disputes

The agreement, performance obligations, breach evidence and any jurisdiction or governing-law clauses should be reviewed together.

Debt recovery and enforcement

Depending on the documents, options may include negotiated resolution, court proceedings or enforcement mechanisms.

Cross-border business matters

International transactions may raise questions of jurisdiction, applicable law, service of documents and enforceability of judgments.

How to prepare the matter

For an initial assessment, collect the official decisions, notices, contracts, reports and correspondence, record the exact dates, and prepare a short chronology. If a document was issued outside Türkiye, it may be necessary to check separately whether an apostille, translation or another formality is required for its use in the relevant Turkish procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide accounting services?

No. Legal work on commercial disputes is not accounting or tax advice.

Can debt be recovered without litigation?

Sometimes negotiated settlement is possible; the practical route depends on the documents and the debtor's position.

Can contracts be discussed in English?

Yes. Client communication and initial legal analysis can be carried out in English.
